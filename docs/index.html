Green parcels are acquisition candidates. Red parcels are federal release candidates.

Use the filters on the left to adjust the ranked list of proposed land swaps. Filter by priority — bridge swaps between patches, state or private landowner contiguity, oil & gas exposure, land value, or the area and distance tolerance between a swap's two parcels. Use the layer toggle in the map's top-right corner to switch between different ownership layers to see how they are distributed across the grasslands.

Ranking Information

Bridge swaps rank first: any proposal that connects two or more separate federal patches outranks all non-bridge proposals.

Bridge swaps are then ranked by Bridge score, which is a 50/50 split of the contiguity gain and the width of the physical connection between patches, each scaled 0–1 by its own max and averaged into one score. The exposure reduction score breaks ties.

Non-bridge swaps are ranked by reduction in exposure which is the receive patch's actual boundary exposure improvement. Contiguity gain breaks ties.

Parcel exposure delta (how much more enclosed the acquired parcel is vs. the released one) is the final tiebreaker for both groups.
